## Cross-cutting results these studies fed

- **Bulk-velocity convergence**: Benedetto (φ0.39, ε6.5), Brancadoro/Tosti (φ0.42, ε6.5), Harajchi (φ0.49, ε8) all give clean-ballast bulk 3.5–3.7 ≈ our pit-ID11 pick 3.45 → the (φ, ε_rock) pair is degenerate in velocity; only coda scattering separates it. Drives the geometry calibration round.
- **Frequency regime**: Couchman + Brancadoro resonance → fouling/grading signal lives near 1–2 GHz; weak at our 400 MHz. Explains the null synthetic slope.
- **Direct pulse**: Benedetto's SRM failure + our old PSO failure → never use direct pulse for ground inference; use it only for system H(f) calibration.
- **Inverse theory → our envelope inversion** (InverseGPR cluster, 15 papers): our measured **d–ε degeneracy IS the textbook non-uniqueness/non-injectivity problem** (Zhu&Li, Scales, Tarantola) → the correct deliverable is the posterior/ridge over (d, ε), not a point estimate. **Concrete posterior recipe = Cordua 2012** (extended Metropolis + sequential-Gibbs priors; DTU SIPPI). Our envelope/W₂ matching is cycle-skip-robust but a 4704-run grid showed **W₂ alone scores a qualitatively wrong model** (matches coda centroid not shape) → combine with a peak/shape term, never W₂ alone.
- **TWO things overturned by the new papers (2026-06-30):** (1) **Single-offset CAN break the d–ε degeneracy via reflection AMPLITUDE** (Xue 2021/Forte 2014 ray-theory: R=A_refl/A_inc → ε contrast → velocity, independent of TWT) — corrects the "structural degeneracy" verdict; our shape-matching never used the amplitude→R→velocity chain. (2) **The coda mismatch is plausibly the SOURCE WAVELET**: we use an ideal Ricker; Jazayeri 2019 (common-offset gprMax+PEST FWI) calls wavelet estimation "the most challenging component that guarantees success," and 2007-CyS gives a method to reconstruct the real emitted wavelet from a trace over known/uniform ground. Estimate the real GSSI wavelet before blaming layering.
- **Toolchain leads:** Jazayeri's **gprMax + PEST** is the established common-offset FWI pipeline (PEST = model-independent optimizer, alternative to our grid); his **3D→2D data transform** is a correction we haven't applied (we compare 2D sim to 3D real). Deep-coda weighting (our t^n gain) ↔ Debye/τ attenuation (Qin); σ weakly observable from one offset → invert ε only.
